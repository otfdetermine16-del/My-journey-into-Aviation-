About Me

My interest in aviation has encouraged me to explore mathematics, physics, navigation, weather, aerodynamics, aircraft systems, technology and the many disciplines that contribute to modern flight.

This portfolio is a record of that development and will grow as I gain knowledge, complete projects, participate in academic activities, develop leadership experience and move toward professional aviation.
